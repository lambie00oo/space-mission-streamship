# Planet image sources

The PNG thumbnails used in the Mission 2 simulation are transparent, resized
derivatives of the local JPEG originals in this directory. Credit remains with
the original NASA image teams:

| Body | Source and credit |
| --- | --- |
| Sun | NASA/GSFC/SDO, AIA 335 image; [Solar Dynamics Observatory](https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/) |
| Earth | NASA Blue Marble, PIA18033; [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA18033) |
| Mars | NASA image S91-32389; [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov/details/S91-32389) |
| Jupiter | NASA/JPL, PIA00358; [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA00358) |
| Saturn | NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute, PIA21047; [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA21047) |
